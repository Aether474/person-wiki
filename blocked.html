Person Wiki is no longer available in Containment Cell 12 or the Site-31 break room.

Following a recent breach of the Person Wiki servers, conducted from Containment Cell 12 territory, as well as massive abuse of the break room microwave, Conference Room B and the guards' trust, we have decided to block access to this website from the following regions: Containment Cell 12, and wherever SCP-PERSON is right now.

Over the last few years we have seen numerous breaches originating from Cell 12. Since the start of the holiday party, these incidents have increased in number and started posing a real threat to the snack table. We cannot accept the fact that these breaches are silently approved, or even encouraged, by the Site Director, who keeps saying it's "kind of a vibe."

As a result, we can no longer provide service to entities that do not respect locked doors, and at the same time pose a threat to Person Wiki itself (SCP-PERSON has edited 4,811 pages).

Although we believe there are good people in Containment Cell 12, our decision is a direct result of recent breach events.
